# Meeting Prep -- Brian Pate Zoom
Date: 2026-03-17 | Time: 3:30 PM ET | Duration: 1 hour

## What This Meeting Is
Not a sales call. A deeper positioning conversation with the goal of:
- Showing Brian something concrete
- Getting clarity on the service vs. product question
- Securing beta access to his 65 brokers

## The One Question Brian Will Judge You On
"What are you actually building?"

Have a clean, specific answer. Not "AI automations." Something like:
- "I'm building a market intelligence agent for boutique brokers -- it pulls MLS data and generates a weekly brief their agents can actually use."
- Or: "I'm building an AI marketing OS that handles the listing pipeline so agents can focus on relationships."

Pick one. Don't hedge.

## Key Things to Carry Into This Meeting

**From Feb 19 -- what Brian told you:**
- "AI is no longer the tool. AI is embedded inside the tool."
- "Their clients are tired of being communicated with by artificial intelligence."
- He values: MLS API, Claude + Excel, statistical mining, backend efficiency
- He doesn't value: AI avatars, mass AI emails, obvious automation gimmicks

**His offer:** 65 brokers, weekly Monday call, 30-day paid beta. This is the goal -- leave today with next steps toward activating that.

## The Fork -- Answer It Today
He asked: "One-on-one custom build, or something they log into?"

Options:
- **Agency/service:** You build custom for each client. Higher margin, slower scale.
- **Productized tool:** SaaS or custom GPT behind paywall. Scalable, harder to build.
- **Hybrid:** Start as a service, productize what works. Probably the right move at this stage.

Come in with a lean -- even if you say "I'm thinking hybrid, here's why" -- don't dodge the question.

## What to Demo (If You Have It)
If you've built anything: show it. Even rough.
If not: walk through a clear concept with a specific workflow. Visuals help.

The goal is to show you've been thinking, building, and that this is real.

## Positioning Line to Land
"AI should free you to be more human."

Use it. It's the framing that addresses his biggest concern (agents burned by obvious AI) while showing you understand the space.

## Questions to Ask Brian
- "What's the biggest operational headache for the brokers on your Monday call?"
- "When you picture a broker using a tool like this daily -- what does that look like?"
- "What would make you comfortable introducing me to your group?"

## What a Win Looks Like
- He says yes to a beta arrangement or sets a date to introduce you to his group
- You leave with one clear next step
- He sees you as credible and focused, not scattered
