# Voice AI Phone Assistant -- Monetization Project

## What This Is
A product where realtors sign up, get a dedicated phone number, and have a persistent AI voice assistant they can call anytime. The AI remembers them, runs automations, and can proactively call them back with updates.

**Tech side:** Handled by Eli's friend. Mac mini server, Twilio, OpenAI Realtime API.

**Eli's job:** Figure out how to package, price, and sell this to realtors.

## Status
`planning` -- friend building the tech, Eli building the business

## Product Concept
- Realtor signs up on a website
- Gets a personal phone number (e.g. "+1-404-XXX-XXXX")
- Calls that number anytime and talks to their AI assistant
- AI remembers their clients, properties, deals, and preferences
- AI can look up property info, draft emails, set reminders
- AI can proactively call realtor with updates

## Target Customer
Independent realtors and small broker teams. The kind of people who:
- Are constantly on the phone
- Struggle to keep track of leads and follow-ups
- Don't have an admin or EA
- Would pay for a tool that saves them 1+ hour/day
