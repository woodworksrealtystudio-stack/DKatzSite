Practice Areas

Comprehensive legal services spanning corporate privacy, cybersecurity, regulatory compliance, and serious criminal defense.

Risk Management & Compliance

Evaluating compliance with U.S. federal and state privacy and information management requirements, including GLBA, HIPAA, HITECH, COPPA, CCPA, GDPR, PCI DSS, state breach notification laws, and other federal and state requirements. Designing privacy programs, advising on organizational structure, policies, and practices.

Commercial & Corporate Transactions

Drafting and negotiating vendor software agreements, technology licensing, data sharing agreements, and consumer-facing privacy policies. Performing privacy and security-based due diligence in mergers and acquisitions, and advising on data integration and governance post-closing.

Investigations & Dispute Resolution

Advocacy in response to allegations of data misuse and representing clients in state and federal investigations, including actions from state attorneys general and the Federal Trade Commission. Counseling on online defamation and social media litigation.

Cyber Advisory & Risk Services

Advising on cyber insurance coverage solutions, reviewing policy language, handling post-breach coverage analysis, and coordinating with specialty brokers. Counseling on cybersecurity consultant retention under attorney-client privilege and conducting risk assessments and security audits.

Integrated Incident Response

Developing incident response plans and conducting tabletop cyber-attack simulations. Counseling on breach containment, incident investigations, consumer notifications, law enforcement communications, regulatory reporting, and litigation readiness.

Criminal Defense

Aggressive defense for serious felony charges throughout Georgia, including violent crimes, drug trafficking, firearm offenses, and high-stakes white-collar matters. Backed by extensive jury trial experience and a former prosecutor's insight into how the government builds its case.
